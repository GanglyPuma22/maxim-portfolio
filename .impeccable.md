# Design Context

## Product
Personal portfolio site for Maxim Mounier.

## Audience
- technical hiring managers
- collaborators
- engineering leads
- curious investors / fundraisers / product partners

## Job to be done
Help someone understand, quickly, that Maxim can build serious systems across aerospace, embedded hardware, full-stack product work, and newer voice/agent workflows — and then let them drill into case studies without slogging through a slide deck.

## Tone / brand personality
- technical but humane
- direct
- credible
- ambitious without startup-bro posturing
- tasteful, not flashy

## Constraints
- local-only prototype for now
- do not expose credentials, guest logins, or private implementation details
- private projects may be showcased, but must be labeled honestly
- desktop-first but responsive
- should be easy to grow into a real deployed site later

## Design direction
Editorial + technical but humane.

Use:
- warm off-white / graphite neutrals
- restrained aerospace-blue accent logic
- strong type hierarchy
- asymmetric composition where it improves focus
- case-study rhythm instead of portfolio-template grids

Avoid:
- dashboard sludge
- generic AI gradients
- glass cards
- equal-weight project tiles everywhere
- fake metrics or fake startup fluff

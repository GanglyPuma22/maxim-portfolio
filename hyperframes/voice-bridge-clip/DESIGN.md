## Style Prompt
Warm technical editorial. The piece should feel like a trustworthy product brief, not cyberpunk neon and not startup fluff. Use the warm cream / navy / slate palette already present in the Voice Bridge architecture graphic, clean card geometry, restrained cobalt accents, and motion that feels deliberate and audit-friendly.

## Colors
- Background cream: `#F4EFE7`
- Panel cream: `#FFFDFC`
- Primary navy: `#294F73`
- Deep slate: `#1F2833`
- Muted slate: `#5D6774`
- Cobalt accent: `#2E6BFF`

## Typography
- Display: `Iowan Old Style, Georgia, serif`
- Body/UI: `Avenir Next, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif`
- Optional data accent: `Courier New, monospace`

## What NOT to Do
- Do not turn it into a black hacker UI.
- Do not use neon gradients or fake waveform clutter.
- Do not bury the message in glassmorphism.
- Do not make it feel playful or toy-like.
- Do not imply autonomous magic; keep the handoff explicit.

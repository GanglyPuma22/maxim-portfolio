## Style Prompt
Cinematic technical documentary. The clip should feel like flight-test infrastructure and simulation operations: dark cockpit interiors, cool blue display light, restrained mission-room typography, and only tiny accent hits from safety-yellow or NASA red. Serious, precise, and public-safe.

## Colors
- Night blue: `#08111D`
- Deep steel: `#122338`
- Cool blue: `#6EA7D8`
- Pale signal: `#D7E7F8`
- Mission white: `#F2F5F8`
- Safety amber: `#D9A441`
- NASA red accent: `#FC3D21`

## Typography
- Display: `Georgia, Times New Roman, serif`
- Body/UI: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`
- Data accent: `Courier New, monospace`

## What NOT to Do
- Do not make it space-movie sci-fi.
- Do not add stars, rockets, or cheesy HUD overload.
- Do not use bright gradients that fight the source photos.
- Do not overclaim non-public details.
- Do not make it feel like a consumer ad; keep it disciplined.
